The City

A capital of the Mughal world

Lahore was one of the great cities of the Mughal Empire. Emperors rebuilt its fort, raised its mosques and laid out its gardens; Sikh and British rulers followed; and in 1940 an open ground beside the old city gave its name to the resolution that led to Pakistan.

Lahore Legacy brings these places together: real photographs, verified dates, points of interest to study, and games that reward what you discover.

Inside the Walls

The Walled City

The old city of Lahore was entered through a ring of gates. From Delhi Gate, the Shahi Guzargah — the royal route — winds past the Wazir Khan Mosque, through bazaars and havelis, to the Lahore Fort. Much of this route has been conserved by the Walled City of Lahore Authority.
